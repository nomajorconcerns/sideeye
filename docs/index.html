Every agent gets the side-eye.

Knows what normal looks like. Notices when it isn't.

SideEye is agent detection and response for AI agents. The goal is to learn what is normal for each agent on your computer, flag what is not, and contain the agent. It is meant to sit beside Cephalo, which blocks dangerous tool calls before they run, but neither requires the other.

What exists today. A desktop console (Windows build tested) running on sample data, and a read-only live view of Cephalo's audit log. Per-agent baselines, anomaly flags and containment are Planned, not built. Nothing is downloadable yet.

What the indicators mean

Shape, word and colour. Never colour alone.

These are the indicators in the console as built. Only the eye changes colour with the fleet's state, and the worst state wins.

The status eye

It sits in the title bar, the navigation, the tray and every pop-up. Hover it for a tooltip; screen readers get a label such as "Fleet status: critical, 2 alerts".

  • CalmBrand green, no badge. Nothing needs you.
  • LearningGrey-blue with three dots. At least one agent is still building its baseline.
  • AlertOrange with a square (a count when large). Open alerts, none critical.
  • ContainedViolet with pause bars. An agent is paused.
  • CriticalRed with a triangle (and the count when large). At least one critical alert.

Order, worst first: Critical, Contained, Alert, Learning, Calm.

Severity badges

  • CriticalFilled triangle. Act now.
  • HighOutlined triangle. Review soon.
  • MediumDiamond. Worth a look.
  • LowCircle. For the record.

Agent status

  • HealthyHealthyInside its baseline.
  • Learning · day 3 of 7LearningBaseline still forming, so it is not judged yet.
  • AnomalousAnomalousActing outside its baseline.
  • ContainedContainedPaused. A decision, not a fault.

Limit: the states in the console come from sample data. Nothing computes them from a real baseline yet, because baselines are Planned.

How to use it

Every screen, and how to do each thing.

A walkthrough of the console as built. Each part says what is real and what is sample data or recording only.

Overview

Start here. It answers: is anything wrong, and where?

  1. Read the four tiles: agents watched, open alerts (split by severity), contained agents (with the median time to contain), and unmanaged agents.
  2. Scan the alert queue. Select a row, or press Enter on it, to open the alert.
  3. Scan "Highest-risk agents": runtime, owner, status, actions per hour against baseline, and a 0 to 100 risk score.

Works on sample data. Nothing is computed from live agents.

Alerts and alert detail

The full queue, and the evidence behind each alert.

  1. Filter by severity, search by text, or filter by agent.
  2. Tick rows and press Mark as expected to dismiss several at once.
  3. Open an alert to see the timeline with absolute times, the baseline comparison (signal, normal, now), a suggested Cephalo policy, and a "If you contain it" blast radius.
  4. Press Contain agent for the Contain dialog, Mark as expected if it was fine, or Apply or Edit first on the suggested policy.

Recording only: Contain and Apply record your choice and update the console. They do not stop an agent or write a Cephalo policy yet. "Revoke http.post" has no action behind it.

Agents and the agent profile

Every agent, and what is normal for it.

  1. Filter by runtime or status, or search by name. Clear filters resets them.
  2. Open an agent. Tabs: Activity, Baseline, Tools & permissions, Alerts, Evidence.
  3. On Baseline, the signal line runs over a shaded band: inside the band is normal for that agent.
  4. Contain agent is in the header and is disabled once the agent is contained.

Sample agents and sample baselines.

Detections

The twelve detections, SE-001 to SE-012.

  1. Each row shows its ID, severity, threshold and 30-day false-positive rate.
  2. Use the toggle to switch a detection on or off. The header counts how many are on.

Only SE-001, 002, 005, 006 and 008 are named in the original plan; the other names, the thresholds and the false-positive rates are placeholders. Thresholds are text, not editable, and toggles are not saved.

Policies

Cephalo policies made from your decisions.

  1. Each policy shows the replay it passed: how many actions it would have blocked, asked about, or left alone across 30 days.
  2. Applying a policy from an alert or from a pop-up makes it active here.
  3. Press Undo to reverse it.

Recording only: no policy file is written to Cephalo, and the replay numbers are sample data.

Evidence

What happened and who decided.

  1. Recent decisions lists every Allow once, Always allow, Block, timeout, Keep, Trust and Report you made.
  2. Framework tiles map exports to EU AI Act Art. 12, 14 and 15, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001 and SOC 2.

The decisions list is real for this run of the app. The exports are sample data, and an export would not make anyone compliant.

Integrations

How SideEye connects to Cephalo.

  1. "Live from Cephalo on this device" shows counts for the last 24 hours (allowed, held, blocked, waiting now) and the 10 newest actions.
  2. It refreshes every 5 seconds while the window is open. The navigation footer says connected or not found.

This is the one real data source. It is read only, and it needs Cephalo on the same machine.

Settings

Appearance, mode, notifications, privacy.

  1. Theme: System, Dark or Light. Also High contrast, Reduce motion, and Text size up to 200%.
  2. Mode: Audit records and alerts only. Enforce also lets SideEye answer Cephalo when it holds an action.
  3. Notifications: toggle critical, high and medium alerts, and Start SideEye at login (off by default).
  4. What SideEye watches lists what it watches and what it never does.

Real: theme and the other appearance options, Mode, and Start at login. Notification toggles and Redact prompts are not wired to anything yet.

The approval pop-up

When Cephalo holds an action and SideEye is in Enforce mode.

  1. A pop-up appears on top of everything, even with the main window closed. One at a time; others show "N more waiting".
  2. Read what was held: the action, where it goes, the agent, and the reason Cephalo gave.
  3. Choose Allow once or Block. Esc blocks. Cmd or Ctrl+Enter confirms.
  4. No answer in 40 seconds blocks it.

Real on Windows for Claude Code, in Enforce mode, but it needs a Cephalo change that has not shipped. The pop-up shows the reason Cephalo gave, not a SideEye baseline, and there is no "Always allow" for real holds.

The Contain dialog

Choosing how to stop an agent.

  1. Pick Pause the agent (preselected), Read-only, Revoke one tool, or Kill.
  2. Add a reason; it is saved to Evidence.
  3. Press the button, whose label follows your choice.

Recording only: it marks the agent Contained in the console and raises the fleet state. It does not enforce anything yet.

The tray and notifications

SideEye keeps running when you close the window.

  1. The tray eye shows the fleet state. Left-click opens a popover with up to three alerts.
  2. Use Up and Down to move, Enter to open an alert, Esc to close. Right-click for Open SideEye and Quit SideEye.
  3. A critical alert shows a Windows notification with Contain and View buttons.

Tray and popover are real on Windows. The notification is confirmed on Windows. macOS and Linux have not been built or tested.

Limit: the console exists and runs, but its fleet, alerts and baselines are sample data. Use this page to learn the interface, not to expect detection.

          Open numbers and roadmap

          What is built, what is planned, what was measured.

          Built

          • Desktop console on sample data (Windows build, tested).
          • Tray icon and always-on-top pop-ups, with the window closed.
          • Read-only live view of Cephalo's log.
          • Approval pop-up for Cephalo's holds (Claude Code, Enforce mode, in development).

          • Per-agent baselines and anomaly flags.
          • Containment that is enforced: pause, kill, quarantine.

          • Tamper protection and integrity checks.
          • Measuring per-action overhead (target under 5 ms). It cannot be measured today: SideEye is not in Cephalo's action path.
