Privacy

This site collects nothing.

  • No cookies, no analytics, no visit counter.
  • Nothing is loaded from other hosts. Fonts, styles and scripts come from this site.
  • No forms. Nothing is sent anywhere.

If you use the page settings, your browser keeps se.theme, se.motion and se.facts in local storage. They never leave your device. Hosting is GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs; we don't receive them.

This covers the website only, not the app.
